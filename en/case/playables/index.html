Mini games instead of banners: people play for 20 seconds and tap "Install"

Before

People scroll past plain banners, and a playable ad must be light and hook at once.

What was done

  • Slingshot Siege: pull the slingshot, smash crates, score points
  • SYNC Survival: a 3D block world, dig and find 5 crystals
  • Built the MEOW DOKU sound kit from the client's video and 8 seamless music loops

The games open in a second and run on any phone.
