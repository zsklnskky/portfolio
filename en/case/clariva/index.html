WhatsApp on autopilot

A guest books on the site, and WhatsApp confirms, reminds and asks for a review by itself

Clariva Spa, Cyprus · Case author: Zakhar Sokolinsky

Before

Admins wrote to every guest by hand: confirm the booking, remind about the visit.

What was done

  • Linked the site's online booking to WhatsApp: the guest instantly gets "You're booked"
  • A reminder comes a day and two hours before, rescheduling right in the chat
  • After the visit the guest is asked for a review
  • All messages use ready templates in Russian, English and Greek

Result

About 190 messages a day go out by themselves, admins no longer write by hand.
