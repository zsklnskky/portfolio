Ads and a report that builds itself

Google and Meta in three languages, and every morning a ready report: spend and bookings

Clariva Spa, Cyprus · Case author: Zakhar Sokolinsky

Before

The spa needed bookings from tourists and locals, but had no ads and no clear stats.

What was done

  • Launched Google search and Meta ads with a campaign per language
  • Set up lead tracking per channel: calls, WhatsApp and online booking
  • The report builds itself from the ad accounts and bookings and arrives as a ready PDF
  • The report shows where spend is over plan and whether the ad payment went through

Result

Ads run on Google and Meta since 21 August 2026, every channel is measured separately.
