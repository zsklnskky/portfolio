34 ad creatives on one board

A matrix of ad directions for a store of eSIMs, games and gift cards

Digital goods store, concept · Case author: Zakhar Sokolinsky

Before

Before launching ads the store needed to compare many visual directions instead of guessing which style would work.

What was done

  • Built a board of 34 mockups in 4 formats by category: eSIM, PlayStation, Xbox and gift cards
  • Each creative is its own colour world: palette, layout and copy, for a fair comparison
  • Built it in live HTML: headlines and prices are real text, hover opens a mockup full size
  • Logo and prices are placeholders, the board is marked as a draft so nobody takes it for final

Result

34 creatives in 4 formats on one screen - a direction is picked in one look. This is the planning stage, no ads ran on it yet, so there are no performance numbers.
