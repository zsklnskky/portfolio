Reels edit themselves

Send a video to the bot and get a finished CapCut project

Telegram bot and CapCut · Case author: Zakhar Sokolinsky

Before

Editing one reel took an hour: cutting pauses, adding subtitles.

What was done

  • Send the video to a Telegram bot or drop it in a folder
  • The bot transcribes speech and cuts the pauses
  • Adds subtitles in the style of a chosen example reel
  • The finished project opens in CapCut, a preview comes to the chat

Result

Five editing steps run with no human involved.
