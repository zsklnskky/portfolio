Viachera, dinner sorted

Plans a week of dinners for your people, budget and "don't eat" list, compares five chains and builds the shopping list

My own product · Case author: Zakhar Sokolinsky

Before

Every evening the same question: what to cook and how much it costs.

What was done

  • A short quiz: how many people, how many days, budget, cuisines and what you don't eat
  • A weekly menu from 524 dishes with portions for your headcount and calories
  • The basket is priced at five chains, Euroopt prices are live
  • Shows where it's cheaper and builds a shopping list by aisle
  • Photos of every dish are AI-generated

Result

The product launched on 20 October 2026.
