Dubai real estate on autopilot

Listings go from the CRM to the portal by themselves, plus decks, stories and retouching

EQT Real Estate, Dubai · Case author: Zakhar Sokolinsky

Before

Agents copied every property from the CRM to Property Finder by hand and made photos and materials themselves.

What was done

  • Properties from Bitrix24 publish to Property Finder and update after every edit
  • Listing Studio: a contract and photos become a finished listing in a minute
  • Made bilingual villa decks for Palm Jumeirah that work on phone and desktop
  • Made a premium-agency story template: Just Listed and Sold
  • Prepared AI-retouched photos: 29 apartment shots and 12 villa shots

55 listings publish with no manual work, six agents share one process.
